# MyPRS TestFlight beta plan

## Test goal

Confirm that a new iPhone user can understand MyPRS, save a real workout, find the resulting Wrapped card, and return for another workout without help.

## First cohort

- Recruit 10–15 people who strength-train at least twice a week.
- Include at least one smaller iPhone, one recent Pro/Max device, and one iPad only if tablet support remains enabled.
- Ask each tester to use MyPRS for seven days and log at least three workouts.
- Offer Founding Tester recognition rather than cash while the product is free.

## TestFlight “What to Test” text

Welcome to the MyPRS beta. Please use the app as you normally would at the gym. Start with onboarding, log a complete workout, open and share your Wrapped card, review Trends, and try reminders. If anything is confusing or fails, take a screenshot and submit feedback through TestFlight. Please do not include private health information in feedback.

## First-session tasks

1. Install and open MyPRS without receiving instructions.
2. Complete or skip each onboarding step as feels natural.
3. Log one real or sample workout containing at least two exercises.
4. Save it and find the new daily Wrapped card.
5. Flip the card, inspect its details, and try sharing it.
6. Find Trends, achievements, reminders, and Account & Backup.
7. Close and reopen the app; confirm the workout is still present.

## Seven-day tasks

- Log at least three workouts.
- Use the app once with a poor or disabled internet connection.
- Enable one reminder and confirm its timing on a real device.
- If using an account, test sign-out/sign-in and backup/restore.
- Report any crash, frozen screen, lost workout, incorrect total, clipped layout, or unclear label.

## Feedback questions

1. What did you think MyPRS was for before logging your first workout?
2. Where, if anywhere, did you hesitate or need help?
3. Did saving a workout feel fast enough to use during training?
4. Was the Wrapped card useful or motivating? Why?
5. What would make you return next week?
6. What single feature felt unnecessary or confusing?
7. Would you recommend MyPRS to another lifter? Why or why not?

## Launch acceptance criteria

- No known crash, frozen launch, duplicated workout, or lost-workout defect.
- At least 80% of testers complete onboarding and save a first workout without help.
- At least 60% log a second workout during the first seven days.
- Every blocker and high-impact usability issue is resolved and retested.
- Crash reporting is configured and a test event is visible in Sentry.
- Privacy and support URLs open publicly on a phone without authentication.
