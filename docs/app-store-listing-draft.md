## Description

Log your training without slowing down your session. MyPRS helps you record lifts, follow personal records, and turn your progress into collectible Wrapped cards.

BUILD YOUR TRAINING HISTORY

- Log exercises, sets, reps, weight, cardio, and session duration.
- Keep your workout history available on your device.
- Review trends and personal-record progress over time.

MAKE PROGRESS FEEL REWARDING

- Generate daily, weekly, and monthly Wrapped cards.
- Unlock achievements, titles, levels, and visual themes.
- Share polished progress cards with friends.

YOUR ACCOUNT IS OPTIONAL

Start logging without creating an account. Create one later if you want cloud backup, restore, or friend challenges. Analytics controls and account deletion are available inside the app.

MyPRS is a training log and motivation tool. It does not provide medical advice.
