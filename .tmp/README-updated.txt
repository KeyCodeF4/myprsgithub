JLEADS - WINDOWS DESKTOP APP

Open JLEADS.exe to start. No installation or browser login is required.

FREE DATA MODE
No account, billing details, or API key is required. Searches use OpenStreetMap
and its community-run Nominatim and Overpass services. Coverage varies and the
free data usually does not include ratings or reviews.

GOOGLE PLACES MODE
Choose Google Places inside the app when you need ratings, review counts,
recent-review signals and more complete contact data. This mode requires your
own Google Places API key and billing must be enabled.

The recent-review option requests Google's reviews field, which is billed at the
Text Search Enterprise + Atmosphere tier. Check current Google Maps Platform
pricing, set a quota and configure billing alerts before running large searches.

NEW LEAD QUALIFICATION FILTERS
- "No / poor website" is one combined filter and is selected by default.
- "No website only" provides a strict list of businesses with no website shown
  on their listing.
- Weak website signals include social/directory/booking-only profiles, free-site
  addresses, invalid links and links that do not use HTTPS.
- "Review in past 90 days" is the default in Google mode.
- "Open businesses only" is the default business-status filter. You can also
  inspect temporarily or permanently closed listings.
- Results and CSV exports include Website status, Business status, Open now,
  Latest review and a Yes/No recent-review field.

Website quality is an automated research signal, not a full design audit. Open
and review each shortlisted website before contacting the business.

Google returns at most five reviews and sorts them by relevance. JLEADS checks
the publication dates among those returned reviews. A positive 90-day match is
reliable, but "Not returned" does not prove that the business has had no recent
reviews.

USING THE APP
- Enter any business type, such as pet groomer or roofing contractor.
- Choose up to five approachable local-business types from the multi-select list.
- Use the 1-400 mile slider to search around Great Bookham, Surrey.
- For ratings and recent reviews, choose Google Places and enter your API key.
- Choose website quality, business status, rating, review-count and review-
  activity filters.
- Choose a maximum output count from 50 to 1,000.
- Select Find leads.
- Double-click a result to open its map listing.
- Use Export CSV to save the filtered call list.

Google mode automatically expands through regional search centres for larger
radii. Free OpenStreetMap mode remains limited to 30 miles. Public OpenStreetMap
services are community infrastructure, so keep free searches modest.

OUTREACH NOTE
Public contact information is not automatic permission to market. Before
calling, verify the business and check applicable TPS/CTPS and UK direct-
marketing rules. Maintain a do-not-call list for anyone who objects.

The lead score is a research shortcut, not a guarantee. Review every prospect.
