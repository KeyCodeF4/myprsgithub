Keep the grind moving.

My workout did not save

Keep the app open and try again. MyPRS is offline-first, so a temporary network problem should not prevent a local workout from being saved.

Restore progress

Open Account, sign in with the same email, then choose Restore. Cloud backup is optional and only restores data previously backed up to that account.

Delete an account

Open Account while signed in and select Delete account. This removes the account and its cloud backup. Local data remains until you reset the app or uninstall it.

Report a bug

Include your phone model, operating-system version, what you tapped, and what you expected to happen. Never send your password.

Contact MyPRS

Email support@myprs.app. We aim to respond within five working days.
