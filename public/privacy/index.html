Privacy policy

Last updated 31 July 2026

MyPRS works without an account. By default, your workouts stay on your phone. We do not sell your data, serve behavioural advertising, or share your workout history with advertisers.

Information stored on your device

Workout entries, exercises, weights, repetitions, sets, Wrapped cards, medals, titles, XP, themes, sound settings and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device.

Optional account and cloud backup

If you choose to create an account, Supabase processes your email address for sign-in and stores the workout and progress data you choose to back up. Creating an account is optional and the main app remains usable without one.

Notifications

Workout reminders are off by default. When enabled, MyPRS asks for notification permission and schedules reminders on your device. We do not use notifications for third-party advertising.

Crash diagnostics

Production versions may use Sentry to receive crash and error diagnostics. Reports may include the error, app action, device type and operating-system version. We configure reports not to include your email, workout contents, exercise names, weights, titles, authentication tokens or advertising identifiers.

Optional usage analytics

Signed-in users can choose to share anonymous product events about onboarding, workout logging, Wrapped creation, sharing and collection features. This is off by default. Events use a random installation identifier and never include your email, exercise names, weights, reps, titles or workout contents. You can withdraw consent from the Account screen at any time.

Sharing

When you share a Wrapped card or medal, MyPRS creates the image on your device and passes it to the app you select. MyPRS does not upload or retain the shared image.

Friend Challenges are private and optional. A challenge stores the selected card type, display name, equipped title, and comparison totals such as volume, duration, estimated calories, repetitions, best weight and PR count. It does not include the exercise list behind the card. Invite links expire after seven days and can be accepted once; both participants can view the result.

Your choices

  • Use MyPRS without an account.
  • Back up and restore manually from the Account screen.
  • Delete your account and cloud data from inside the app.
  • Remove local data by resetting or uninstalling the app.
  • Ask for access to or deletion of data associated with your account.

Children

MyPRS is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us so we can remove it.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email support@myprs.app.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update the date above. Material changes to how personal data is handled will also be highlighted in the app.
